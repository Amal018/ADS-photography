# Surface brief: ADS Photography marketing site

Scope: whole marketing site (Home, Portfolio, Packages, Corporate, About, Blog, Testimonials, Contact). Mode: Persuade on Home, Packages, Corporate and Contact; Experience on Portfolio; Read on Blog.

Audience: Coimbatore families planning weddings, maternity and family shoots (mostly on phones), plus local businesses needing product shoots and retainers. Action: book a shoot (form or WhatsApp) or request a corporate quote. Proof: none real yet; every photo, testimonial, price and bio is a labelled placeholder. Constraints: English only, mobile-first, LCP < 2.5s.

Direction history: the first build (hand-painted signboard, seed d8cd6967) was rejected by the user as "like a cartoon". A restrained Bodoni/neutral build followed. The user was then shown five professional themes and chose Theme 4, "Modern Swiss".

## Direction contract

THESIS: Modern Swiss. A crisp, corporate studio site on a strict grid where photographs lead. It refuses ornament, rounded softness and decorative theming.

OWN-WORLD: Pure white ground (#ffffff), light grey bands (#f3f3f3), black ink (#0b0b0b), 1px hairline rules (#e2e2e2), a black band for closing calls to action and the corporate page headers. One family throughout: Archivo, with headings at 600, uppercase, tracking -0.035em. Square black buttons (primary) and square outline buttons (secondary); no rounded corners anywhere. Neutral grey photo slots until real work arrives.

STORY: The visitor sees "Photography studio in Coimbatore" beside a three-image mosaic. They scan what the studio photographs and the selected work, understand the two divisions and three packages, then book (form or WhatsApp) or request a corporate quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two columns on desktop. The large uppercase Archivo H1 is on the left, with the value line, Book a shoot (primary) and WhatsApp (secondary), and small text links. The image mosaic is on the right: one tall image and two squares. On mobile, the text comes first and the mosaic follows.

FORM: User-selected Theme 4 "Modern Swiss" from a five-theme round, after rejecting seed d8cd6967. Code-led. SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the hero headline and images rise into place once on load; photos scale 2.5% on hover; nav items draw an underline. Motion: short, ease-out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: real photos, logo, contact details, prices and inclusions, founder bios, testimonials.
