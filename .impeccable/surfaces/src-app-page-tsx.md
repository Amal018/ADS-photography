# Surface brief: ADS Photography marketing site

Scope: whole marketing site (Home, Portfolio, Packages, Corporate, About, Blog, Testimonials, Contact). Mode: Persuade on Home, Packages, Corporate and Contact; Experience on Portfolio; Read on Blog.

Audience: Coimbatore families planning weddings, maternity and family shoots (mostly on phones), plus local businesses needing product shoots and retainers. Action: book a shoot (form or WhatsApp) or request a corporate quote. Proof: none real yet; every photo, testimonial, price and bio is a labelled placeholder. Constraints: English only, mobile-first, LCP < 2.5s.

Direction history: the first build (hand-painted signboard, seed d8cd6967) was rejected by the user as "like a cartoon". They asked for a professional design. This is the standing exit: the category standard, played straight.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet, professional studio site where the photographs lead and the interface recedes. It refuses decorative theming and illustrative chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral paper ground (#fafaf8), stone bands (#f0efec), near-black ink, fine 1px rules (#dcdbd6), a night band (#121211) for closing calls to action and the corporate page headers. Bodoni Moda display (regular, not italic) for headings. Hanken Grotesk for text and UI. Pill buttons: solid ink for primary, outline for secondary. Neutral photo slots with a small camera caption until real work arrives.

STORY: The visitor sees "Photography studio in Coimbatore" beside a three-image mosaic. They scan what the studio photographs and the selected work, understand the two divisions and three packages, then book (form or WhatsApp) or request a corporate quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two columns on desktop. The large Bodoni H1 is on the left, with the value line, Book a shoot (primary) and WhatsApp (secondary), and small text links. The image mosaic is on the right: one tall image and two squares. On mobile, the text comes first and the mosaic follows.

FORM: The category standard (canon), chosen by the user after rejecting seed d8cd6967. Code-led. SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the hero headline and images rise into place once on load; photos scale 2.5% on hover; nav items draw an underline. Motion: short, ease-out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: real photos, logo, contact details, prices and inclusions, founder bios, testimonials.
